# JUDGMENT AND SENTENCE

NOW on this ___ day of __________, 2025, the above-named Defendant appearing in person and with counsel, Thomas Harrington, and the State appearing by Assistant District Attorney Robert McAdams, the Court finds as follows:

The Defendant previously entered a plea of guilty to the offense charged herein. The Court, having reviewed the plea agreement, the Presentence Investigation, statements of counsel, the allocution of the Defendant, and the interests of justice, hereby enters Judgment and Sentence.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that the Defendant, Jill Margaret Voss, is sentenced as follows:

- Three Hundred Sixty-Five (365) days in the Tillman County Jail;
- Ninety (90) days suspended;
- Two Hundred Seventy (270) days to serve;
- Credit for time previously served as allowed by law.

Upon release from incarceration, the Defendant shall be placed on supervised probation for a period of twelve (12) months under the supervision of the Oklahoma Department of Corrections and any transferred supervising authority approved by the Court.

The Court specifically finds that intensive supervision is necessary due to the Defendant’s demonstrated issues involving judgment, impulse control, and compliance with lawful authority.

The Defendant shall comply with all standard conditions of supervision and all special conditions imposed herein.

Failure to comply with any condition may result in sanctions, modification of supervision, custodial remand, revocation proceedings, or imposition of the suspended sentence.

SO ORDERED.

# INTENSIVE SUPERVISION ADDENDUM

The following additional restrictions and procedures shall apply during the intensive supervision phase of probation:

### COMPLIANCE CONTACTS

1. Defendant shall answer the residence door immediately during compliance checks.
2. Repeated delay in answering the door may be interpreted as evasion or noncompliance.
3. Compliance checks may occur at any hour deemed appropriate by supervising officers.
4. Defendant shall present herself fully and truthfully during all compliance contacts.

---

### CURFEW AND MOVEMENT

5. Defendant shall remain inside the approved residence during curfew hours absent prior authorization.
6. Overnight travel requires prior written approval.
7. Defendant shall provide schedules and itinerary information upon request.

---

### RESIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

8. Defendant shall maintain a stable and orderly residence.
9. Defendant shall not permit prohibited persons to reside in or routinely visit the residence.
10. Defendant shall immediately disclose changes in romantic, domestic, or custodial living arrangements.

---

### SANCTIONS

11. Technical violations may result in graduated sanctions including:

- increased reporting,
- expanded curfew,
- GPS activation,
- home confinement,
- custodial sanctions,
- extension recommendations,
- revocation proceedings.

12. Repeated violations may be treated as willful noncompliance.
13. Supervising officers may recommend immediate custodial remand for serious violations.

---

### ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF STRICT COMPLIANCE

14. Defendant acknowledges that intensive supervision is intended to impose substantial structure and accountability.
15. Defendant acknowledges that technical violations may result in incarceration even absent new criminal conduct.
