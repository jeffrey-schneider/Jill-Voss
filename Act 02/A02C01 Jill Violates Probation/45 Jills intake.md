The van rolled through the Tillman County sally port just after dusk.

Jill knew the angle of the turn before it happened.

The slight drop in pavement.

The metal vibration through the bench seat.

The smell.

County jail always smelled faintly damp after sunset. Concrete holding old humidity beneath disinfectant and bleach.

Nobody in the transport compartment spoke.

The other women sat where they had been chained. Hands low. Eyes tired. One cried silently without wiping her face. Another stared at the floor hard enough to disappear into it.

The rear doors opened.

“Stand up.”

The deputy’s voice was not cruel. Just practiced.

One by one the women shuffled out in linked restraints.

Jill stepped down onto painted concrete.

Tillman County Jail.

Again.

“Step forward.”

The officer checked paperwork.
