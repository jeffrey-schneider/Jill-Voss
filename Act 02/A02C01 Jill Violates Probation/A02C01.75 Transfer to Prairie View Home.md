At 0400 hours the lights exploded on.

“ROLL IT UP!”

Jill jerked awake instantly.

Boots hammered concrete outside the pod.

“PVH TRANSFERS! MOVE!”

Her pulse accelerated immediately.

Women climbed down from bunks half asleep, already moving faster than normal county rhythms.

Because everyone understood:  
this was different.

Jill rolled her mattress automatically.

Green uniform.  
Boots.  
Hands shaking only slightly.

The deputy pointed.

“Property in hand. Line up.”

No breakfast yet.

No coffee.

Only movement.

One by one the women were cuffed.

Side cuffs first.

Then chained together for transport staging.

Jill looked down the line briefly.

Fear everywhere.

No talking.

Outside, somewhere beyond the jail walls, Oklahoma remained dark and cold.

And somewhere out there…

Prairie View House was waiting.
