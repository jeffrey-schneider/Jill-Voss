Jill remained seated quietly across from her.

“Marla?”

A small movement beneath the blanket.

“Can I call you Marla?”

No answer for a second.

Then:  
“…everybody else does.”

Jill nodded once.

“Pretty name.”

Marla looked faintly confused by that.

The room stayed quiet except for distant ventilation and the soft shuffle of Deputy Martin finishing count near the officer station.

Jill picked carefully through the next words.

“Will you sit by me at breakfast tomorrow?”

Marla blinked slowly.

“Why?”

Jill looked down briefly at her own hands.

“Because everybody’s still mad at me about the haircut thing.”

That almost sounded childish once spoken aloud.

But Jill continued anyway.

“I think maybe they hate me a little.”

Marla stared at her from the bunk.

“You got us shaved.”

“I know.”

The answer came immediately.

Jill swallowed once.

“I’m trying really hard not to screw up in here anymore.”

Another long pause.

Then Jill said quietly:  
“I’m kind of lonely.”

Something in Marla’s expression shifted slightly at that. Not improvement exactly. Recognition.

Jill leaned back against the cold concrete wall beside the neighboring bunk.

“Can you help me?”

Marla frowned faintly.

“With what?”

“With not eating breakfast alone.”

Silence again.

Then, softer:  
“And maybe… maybe forgive me a little.”

Marla stared at the underside of the bunk above her for several seconds.

When she finally spoke, her voice sounded less hollow.

“I don’t know if I’m very good company right now.”

Jill answered honestly.

“I don’t think either of us is.”

That did it.

Not dramatically.

But enough.

Marla slowly sat up for the first time since lights-out began.

The blanket slipped from around her shoulders.

“I’ll sit with you,” she said quietly.

Across the pod Deputy Martin turned off the final overhead row without interrupting them.

For the first time all day, Marla Reed looked connected to the room again instead of disappearing inside it.

And Jill, without fully realizing it yet, had just done exactly what Counselor Avery had been hoping somebody in Pod Two would do.
