After the haircuts, Voss was reassigned to the rear of the movement line.

Deputy Keller looked down the row before speaking.

“When movement returns to Pod Two, you will strip out and form shower line in alphabetical order. Three offenders per rotation. You will shower, dry, and receive replacement uniform issue.”

He paused briefly.

“No talking during processing. No delays. No deviations from assigned order.”

The offenders remained silent.

“When your rotation is complete, you will stand by your assigned bunk until dress-out is authorized for the entire pod. Nobody dresses early. Nobody leaves position without instruction.”

Keller looked once toward the back of the line where Jill now stood.

“Tracking?”

“Yes sir,” the line answered unevenly.

“Move.”
