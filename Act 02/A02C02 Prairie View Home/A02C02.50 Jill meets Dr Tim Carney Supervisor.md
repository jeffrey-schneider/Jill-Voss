
Jill walks into Dr. Tim Carney's outer office. His administrative assistant sees Jill is dressed in B&W's with a cap.

"Name?"

"Voss, Jill Margaret."

"Appointment time?"

"1500 hours, ma'am."

"You're early. Just right. To be on time is to be late."

"Yes ma'am."

"I'll inform Dr. Carney you are here. Stand Voss. And since you are a first monther, face the wall."

Jill realized that some things would follow her wherever she went.

"Yes ma'am."

"Voss, did I ask you a question or speak to you?"

"No ma'am."

"Then keep silent."

Jill stands, looking at the wall.  1530 hours and the phone rings. "Voss, Jill Margaret. Yes sir. I will send her in."

She hangs up. "The doctor will see you now."

Jill walks to the door and knocks three times.

"Come."

Jill takes a breath and walks in.

"Ms. Voss. Remain standing. What time was our appointment?"

"1500 hours, sir."

"Okay. You were early. Very good. I made you wait 30 minutes. I almost left for the day."

Jill said nothing.

Dr. Carney retrieved her file. There were several pages in it already. Dr. Carney frowned as he leafed through the documents. He took a deep breath.

"Face the wall, Voss. Pay attention, I will only give this to you once. Why are you here?"

"Sir, I was disciplined several times for my hair." Jill replied.

"Several times? Three times, Voss. Three times we had to remind you about proper hair length. You are lucky you are new here. You get one reminder. The second one requires action. What did they tell you about hair length, Voss?"

Jill explained her understanding of the rules.  

"Sounds correct. What do you not understand about these rules?"

"I'm sorry, sir. No excuses."

"Voss, you have been here less than two days and you have met with me already."

Dr. Carney lifted the phone and dialed a number, then clicked on the speaker button. 

Jill could hear the rings. On the third ring, the line was picked up. "Barber."

"Mr. Jones. When is the first time you can get an entire pod in at one time for haircuts?"

"Tomorrow at 1300 hours, sir."

"Schedule tomorrow 1300 hours for full haircuts, close regulation for pod 2. Thank you Mr. Jones."

"Twelve short regulation hair cuts. Scheduled." and he hung up.

Jill was sure this was a joke, she almost smiled. Then Carney dialed another number and put it on speaker. "Tammi? Pod 2 has an appointment tomorrow. Barber shop. Close regulation. All of them."

"Yes sir. All offenders in pod 2 haircuts. 1300 hours tomorrow. Any reason you'd like listed?"

"Voss, Jill Margaret. I am pretty sure she will watch her hair from now on."

"I'll tag it sir. Anything else?"

"No, Tammi. You are as efficient as always. Good day."

"Voss, let this teach you."

"Yes sir."

"Are we tracking, Voss?"

"Tracking sir."

"Dismissed."
